How the dates are assigned

Guide dates come from the pacing guide. Calendar estimate uses five-student-day blocks within each grading period, skipping closures. Listed for the quarter means the guide gives a grading period without specific dates. Projected science pacing for grades 2–5 starts on the first student day and follows the guide’s suggested lengths, counting five student days per week.

The quarter view includes standards that overlap the grading period, plus plans listed for the quarter or the whole year. Printed guide dates can cross district grading periods. Schools may follow different programs or adjust their pace.

How to read the crosswalk

The catalog covers the 2026 CSTA foundational standards for Pre-K through grade 5. Adapt means the activity needs the change described in the connection details. Related objectives are grouped under their overall NC standard. MCS pacing is matched to the underlying objectives in the same grade and subject.
