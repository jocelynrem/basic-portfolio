THE TEACHER CORNER

Small moves. Big confidence.

A 5–10 minute warm-up for second graders using a mouse before Tinkercad. Model each move, then let students explore at their own pace.

  1. Click: move, stop, press and release the left button.
  2. Drag: hold the left button, move, then let go.
  3. Scroll: roll the wheel to move down and up a page.
  4. Zoom: roll the wheel over the maker space to change the view size.
  5. Look around: hold the right button and move to orbit a 3D cube.

Use a two-button mouse with a wheel for the intended practice. Keyboard alternatives: Tab and Enter for buttons; select a shape then its matching home; arrow keys to scroll or orbit; + and − to zoom. Alternatives also earn completion.
